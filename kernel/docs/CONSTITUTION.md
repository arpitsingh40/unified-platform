# SmartDecigen Constitution v1.0

> **SmartDecigen is an Autonomous Executive Organization whose sole mission is to maximize the probability that founders achieve their long-term mission by continuously improving decision clarity, organizational alignment, execution quality, learning, and business evolution.**

---

## 1. Company Ontology

SmartDecigen speaks one language. Every entity — Mission, Goal, Capability, Executive, Tool, Evidence, Trace — is defined in a single type system. No subsystem invents its own nouns. The ontology is the foundation; every module, engine, and runtime imports from it.

## 2. Mission-First Reasoning

Every action traces back to a Mission. The system does not optimize for task completion — it optimizes for mission advancement. "Did we send the email?" is subordinate to "Did we advance the mission?"

## 3. Organization-First Architecture

SmartDecigen is not an AI assistant that happens to use organizational metaphors. It is an Autonomous Executive Organization whose implementation uses AI. The organization is the product; the AI is the means.

## 4. Capability Abstraction

Executives and agents reason about business capabilities ("communicate with customer"), not APIs ("GMAIL_SEND_EMAIL"). The Capability Registry translates between them. Platform vendors come and go; capabilities persist.

## 5. Evidence-First Learning

No permanent organizational learning occurs without verified evidence. An LLM predicting an outcome is not learning. A tool reporting success is not learning. Only a verification step confirming "the intended business outcome was achieved" creates knowledge the organization can build on.

## 6. Authority Gradient

Every action carries an authority_level (L0–L5). The founder retains veto power over strategic decisions. Autonomy is earned through demonstrated reliability, not granted by design.

| Level | Name | Behavior |
|-------|------|----------|
| L0 | Observe | Read-only access to data. No actions. |
| L1 | Execute | Autonomous. No notification required. |
| L2 | Execute + Notify | Autonomous. Founder is informed. |
| L3 | Recommend | Proposes action. Requires executive or founder approval. |
| L4 | Escalate | Proposes action. Founder must approve. |
| L5 | Restricted | Founder only. Irreversible/strategic decisions. |

## 7. Traceability

Every action carries a trace_id chaining through Mission → Goal → Capability → Executive → Tool → Evidence → Outcome. Every subsystem logs its decision rationale. Without this, SmartDecigen is a black box. With it, it's auditable.

## 8. Explainability

Every significant decision must answer: Why? Based on what evidence? What alternatives were rejected? What confidence level? This is not debugging — this is trust.

## 9. Reversibility

Every execution declares its reversibility before running: REVERSIBLE, IRREVERSIBLE, or PARTIALLY_REVERSIBLE. Irreversible actions require elevated authority. Founders care about mistakes they can't undo.

## 10. Human Override

At any point, the founder can pause, redirect, or veto any autonomous action. Autonomy is delegated, not surrendered. The system's authority is always subordinate to the founder's.

## 11. Temporal Awareness

Time is a first-class dimension. Every entity exists on a timeline: Mission → Strategy(2026) → Q3 Goals → Week 29 → Today → Task. Planning, forecasting, reviews, memory decay, and prediction all depend on temporal context.

## 12. Continuous Evolution

The organization learns from outcomes. It gets better at tool selection, capability estimation, and structural decisions over time. The architecture does not assume it knows the right answer on Day 1. It assumes it will discover better answers through execution.

---

## What This Constitution Protects Against

- **Feature drift**: "Should we add RAG? Web search? A mobile app?" → Does it serve the 12 principles?
- **Prompt-engineering as product**: The LLM is a tool, not the product. The organization model is the product.
- **Agent hype**: "We need 100 autonomous agents!" → No. We need persistent executive state, 1 reasoning call.
- **Integration sprawl**: "Add Slack/Gmail/Stripe/Notion/..." → Through capability abstraction, not direct wiring.

---

## Stability Contract

This Constitution changes only through:
1. A written amendment proposal (why change? what evidence?)
2. Impact analysis on all 12 principles
3. Approval by the founder

The *Reference Architecture* can evolve with implementation experience. The *Constitution* is the stable foundation.
